# References

The papers behind the algorithms of remblais, all inherited from
[GeomLoss](https://www.kernel-operations.io/geomloss/).

- **Sinkhorn divergences, Hausdorff divergence.** J. Feydy, T. Séjourné,
  F.-X. Vialard, S. Amari, A. Trouvé, G. Peyré. *Interpolating between Optimal
  Transport and MMD using Sinkhorn Divergences.* AISTATS 2019, PMLR 89,
  pp. 2681–2690. [proceedings.mlr.press](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v89/feydy19a.html)
- **Unbalanced transport (`reach`).** T. Séjourné, J. Feydy, F.-X. Vialard,
  A. Trouvé, G. Peyré. *Sinkhorn Divergences for Unbalanced Optimal
  Transport.* 2019. [arXiv:1910.12958](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.12958)
- **Image barycenters (`ImagesBarycenter`).** H. Janati, M. Cuturi,
  A. Gramfort. *Debiased Sinkhorn barycenters.* ICML 2020, PMLR 119,
  pp. 4692–4701. [proceedings.mlr.press](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v119/janati20a.html)
- **ε-scaling and multiscale transport.** B. Schmitzer. *Stabilized Sparse
  Scaling Algorithms for Entropy Regularized Transport Problems.* SIAM Journal
  on Scientific Computing, 2019. [doi:10.1137/16M1106018](https://doi.org/10.1137/16M1106018),
  [arXiv:1610.06519](https://arxiv.org/abs/1610.06519)
- **The GeomLoss implementation.** J. Feydy. *Geometric data analysis, beyond
  convolutions.* PhD thesis, Université Paris-Saclay, 2020.
- **Kernel norms (MMD).** A. Gretton, K. M. Borgwardt, M. J. Rasch,
  B. Schölkopf, A. Smola. *A Kernel Two-Sample Test.* JMLR 13(25),
  pp. 723–773, 2012. [jmlr.org](https://jmlr.org/papers/v13/gretton12a.html)
- **KeOps, the predecessor of kernox.** B. Charlier, J. Feydy, J. A. Glaunès,
  F.-D. Collin, G. Durif. *Kernel Operations on the GPU, with Autodiff,
  without Memory Overflows.* JMLR 22(74), pp. 1–6, 2021. [jmlr.org](https://jmlr.org/papers/v22/20-275.html)
